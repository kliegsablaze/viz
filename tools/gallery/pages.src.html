Viz pages: Sequence and Mod (mockup)

The black screen is the Move's display, pixel for pixel, in Schwung's own label font. Turn the eight knobs under it (or scroll over a cell). The Matrix on the right plays what the pages say, to the gallery's drum loop.
120 BPM
  • Sequence page. STEP picks which of the 8 steps the other knobs edit. Each step has a scene, a length in BARS (Off skips it), and its own COLOR, RATE, SIZE and FOLD, each of which can be Same (follow the Look page). PLAY turns the sequence on. While Move plays, the steps follow the song's bars from bar 1, so the same bar always shows the same step.
  • Mod page. Two LFOs, one a row, as on Strut's Mod view: KIND (Sine, Ramp, Square, Random, Kick, Level), RATE (1/16 to 16 bars, on the beat), AIM (what it moves), DEPTH (how far, and which way). They never turn a knob: the knob keeps what you set, and the picture moves around it.
  • The pictures follow Strut and Quilt: one-pixel lines, small and centred, and only time controls move. SCENE draws the scene itself as an 8x8 grid of dots. Page 1's knobs get the same pictures, so the Look page comes almost for free.
